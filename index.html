How it works

  • Pick a full 15, one per position: 1 loosehead prop, 2 hooker, 3 tighthead prop, 4 & 5 locks, 6 blindside, 7 openside, 8 number 8, 9 scrum-half, 10 fly-half, 11 left wing, 12 inside centre, 13 outside centre, 14 right wing, 15 full-back.
  • Captain: his points count double.
  • Supersub: one extra pick who starts on the bench. His points count triple. If he's named to start instead, he gets only half points (same as the official Six Nations fantasy game).
  • Max 3 players from any one nation, counting your Supersub.
  • “Position TBC” players can fill any forward or back slot.
  • Deadline: kick-off of the round's first match. Round 4 closed at Ireland v Argentina, Fri 6 Nov, 20:10 UK, so no more Round 4 sides can be sent. For a later round, post the side in the WhatsApp group IRL Team and Weekend plans before that round's first kick-off. Swaps: a player could be changed until his own match kicked off.
